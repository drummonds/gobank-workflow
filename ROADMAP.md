# Roadmap

## v0.1 — Foundation

- Daily interest accrual workflow (driven by go-luca ledger)
- End-of-day processing pipeline
- Configurable scheduling (cron-style or interval-based)
- go-workflows integration for durable, resumable workflows

## Future

- Payment settlement workflows
- Account opening / closing lifecycle
- Regulatory reporting generation
- Retry and compensation patterns for failed steps
