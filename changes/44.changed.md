The instances queue lists top-level runs only: a run with children is found once and its children are read on its page, so bytestone-scan's batches are no longer buried under their scans and photos. A filter naming a purpose, workflow, revision or key lists children too, since the operator asked for them by name; `status` and `since` alone stay top level. `RunFilter.TopLevel` is the store side: only the runs with no parent
