A definition states its place among parents and children. `Step.Many` becomes `Step.Mode`: unset starts one child, `OneAtATime` a loop (the next child when the previous is through, or when an operator starts it), `AllAtOnce` a fan-out; breaking for hosts that set `Many`. `Step.Given` is prose for what a child is given, since the engine hands it only its key and business date, and `Step.Joins` on a later step names the child-starting step whose children it waits for. The parent's definition page says of each child-starting step how it starts its children, what it gives them and which step waits for all, warning when a fan-out has no join; the child's page says who starts it, how, and given what.
